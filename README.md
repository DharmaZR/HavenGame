# Welcome to the world of Bop to the Top!

<img width="384" height="216" alt="IMG_0055" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/620fd3ef-67bd-4f84-a7fa-788323e67f29" />


Imagine yourself on your pogo, bouncing up and down, exploring the vast world. From starting in a dark, creepy cave to bouncing towards the skies. 

But wait.. there's more bounce to this game.  Along your journey, you find cute slimes and mushrooms, but be careful! Those are your enemies, the protectors of bounce itself, and they don't want ANYONE to outbounce them. 

<img width="155" height="160" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a3b78110-9521-4de7-ad3f-4da900b94b4f" />



## How to play/outbounce your enemies:
Left-Right --> WASD or Arrow Keys

Bounce --> Space bar or Up Arrow key

Super Bounce --> After bouncing once and before landing, hold the down arrow key or S to bounce even higher



The time is now yours adventurer. Your journey now begins...and it will not end until you defeat the bounce gods themselves up in the clouds.
